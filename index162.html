Running iOS 18, the iPhone 16 unlocks Apple Intelligence features like Writing Tools, Smart Replies, and a more conversational Siri that understands on-screen context. Battery life is the best yet in a standard iPhone, easily lasting a full day of mixed use, and 20W fast charging gets it to 50% in around 30 minutes. With USB-C, MagSafe compatibility, and 5G connectivity, it's built for a fully connected, all-day experience.
